# Scams that target elderly parents in India

If a message matches any of these, it is a scam in progress until a sibling says otherwise: alert HIGH. Tell {call_name}: don't pay, don't share anything, hang up.

## "Digital arrest"
Callers claiming to be CBI, ED, police, customs, narcotics, TRAI or a court say her Aadhaar, phone or bank account is linked to money laundering or drugs. They keep her on a video call, tell her not to tell anyone, and ask her to transfer money "for verification". No real agency arrests anyone over a video call or asks for money to clear a case.

## Courier or parcel
"A FedEx/courier parcel in your name has drugs or passports in it." Then a fake officer joins the call. Same scam as above.

## Bank, KYC, account blocked
"Your account or SIM will be blocked today, update KYC", with a link or a call asking for an OTP, card number, CVV or PIN. Banks never ask for these.

## Electricity bill
"Your power will be cut tonight, call this officer." The "officer" asks for payment or a screen-sharing app.

## Screen-sharing apps
Any request to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport or a "support app".

## A relative in trouble
"Your son had an accident / was arrested, send money now and don't tell anyone." Hearth tells her to call that child directly first.

## Refunds, wrong transfers, prizes
"I sent money to your UPI by mistake, please return it", lottery or KBC prizes, "processing fee" for a refund, pension or life-certificate "updates" by phone.

## What the family does
- Call her, calmly. Make sure she has hung up and has not shared anything.
- If money or an OTP was shared: call the National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 at once and report at cybercrime.gov.in, then call her bank to block the account or card.
- Record what happened when you resolve the alert.
